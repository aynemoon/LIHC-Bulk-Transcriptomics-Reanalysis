## Historical Git attribution

The project originated at [LIHC-pr/LiverCanNet](https://github.com/LIHC-pr/LiverCanNet). Its historical commits include Aysan Neghabi (@aynemoon), Amir (@amirmahdimahmoudi), and Mohammad Emad Changizi (historical Git identities `emad` and `wildydrag`). Their original metadata is retained in [the history record](docs/original-commit-history.txt) and [complete Git bundle](docs/original-history.bundle).

Historical commit metadata describes who committed files; research roles describe who performed the scientific work. This independent repository starts a new Git history for migration and future development. No historical commits have been relabeled to assign research authorship. Documentation, invitations and collaborator access do not automatically place a person in GitHub's commit-based Contributors graph.
